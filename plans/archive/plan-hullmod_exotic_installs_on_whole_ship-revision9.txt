WHY STRICT FIRES DURING REFIT (root cause)
------------------------------------------
getWorkModeOptional() (HullmodExoticHandler.kt:1083-1091) resolves the work mode as:
    runningFromRefitScreen()        -> LENIENT
    runningFromExoticaTechnologiesScreen() -> STRICT
    fallback (OUTSIDE default)      -> STRICT
STRICT expects the exotic's marker hullmod to ALREADY be present on the variant (hasHullMod gate,
HullmodExoticHandler.kt:271-279) and demands the variant be instance-identical to what was expected.
LENIENT (refit) tolerates hullVariantId churn. Any dialog on top of the refit flips
runningFromRefitScreen() to false while runningFromExoticaTechnologiesScreen() (hasOptions) still
reads true -> workMode silently becomes STRICT mid-refit -> the remove flow bails at the gate.
STRICT is correct for the planetside "exoticatech" screen (marker already written there) and stays
UNTOUCHED. LENIENT is the refit contract and must tolerate clone churn on both shouldInstall (already
hullId-based) and shouldRemove/isInstalledOn (currently strict instance contains).
