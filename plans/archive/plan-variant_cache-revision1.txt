## Phase 1 — EVICTION LOG POLISH (behavior unchanged)
- Differentiate eviction log lines:
    "VariantTagProvider.get | EVICTED EXPIRED | member=.. days=N | cache.size = M"
    "VariantTagProvider.get | EVICTED CLOCK-UNAVAILABLE | member=.. | cache.size = M"
    "VariantTagProvider | SWEEP EVICTED member=.. days=N\tcache.size: M"
    "VariantTagProvider | SWEEP EVICTED CLOCK-UNAVAILABLE member=..\tcache.size: M"
- elapsedSince() keeps the non-null Long return and the EVICT_FROM_CACHE_DAYS numeric sentinel
  (semantics unchanged). Call sites keep the single Long comparison against the TTL and only
  branch on the log label: elapsed == EVICT_FROM_CACHE_DAYS -> CLOCK-UNAVAILABLE, else
  EXPIRED days=N. Sentinel = 2*CACHE_TTL_GAME_DAYS (=4): first genuine expiry floors to days=3,
  never collides. (Residual collision at floored 4 requires surviving a full day past expiry
  without any get()/sweep evicting and re-writing the entry — practically unreachable.)
- Logs are added NOW and used as validation data from here on; Phase 4 only re-collects.
