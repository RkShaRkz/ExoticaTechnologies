## Clarifying Implementation Details
- The canonical `fuzzyVariantIdEquals` uses Kotlin `substringBeforeLast("_")` on both ids,
  which is equivalent to the old Java `lastIndexOf('_')` comparison for real hullVariantIds
  (ids without an underscore degrade to an exact comparison).
- The merge of `treeContains` keeps the two call modes explicit: exact (`isFuzzy == false`)
  for disambiguation and fuzzy (`isFuzzy == true`) for prefix matching.
